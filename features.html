Feature list

Barcode stock intake

Scan an item with a phone camera and StockPilot fills in the product name, size, supplier and last price paid. New lines take about four seconds each.

Core

Low stock alerts

Set the level you never want to fall below. StockPilot watches every sale and messages you before a line runs out, not after a customer asks for it.

Core

Supplier orders

Low lines are grouped by supplier into a draft purchase order. Edit the quantities, send it as a PDF, and stock updates itself when the delivery is checked in.

Core

Sales and waste reports

See which lines earn their shelf space and which sit there. Weekly reports show your best and slowest sellers, plus what you wrote off and why.

Reporting

Staff accounts

Give each staff member their own login. Casuals can count and receive stock, managers can change prices and place orders. Every change is logged against a name.

Team

Offline first

Back rooms and cool rooms have terrible reception. StockPilot keeps working with no signal and syncs everything the moment your phone finds wi-fi again.
