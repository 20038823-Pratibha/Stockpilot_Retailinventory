How it works

Three steps, then it runs itself

  1. 01

    Scan your stock in

    Point a phone camera at a barcode and StockPilot pulls the product name, size and supplier. No typing, no spreadsheet templates to fill.

  2. 02

    Set a reorder point

    Tell StockPilot the level you never want to drop below. It watches every sale and messages you the moment a line gets close.

  3. 03

    Send the order

    Your low lines are grouped by supplier into a draft order. Check it, change it, send it. The stock updates itself when the delivery arrives.

Built for the shop floor

Made for people who are already busy

Most inventory software is written for warehouses with a stock manager. StockPilot is written for the person who is also serving customers, signing for deliveries and closing up. Everything happens in a few taps, on the phone already in your apron pocket.

  • Works offline and syncs when the wi-fi comes back
  • Reads your existing barcodes, no relabelling
  • Staff accounts with separate permissions
