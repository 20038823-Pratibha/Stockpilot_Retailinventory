About us

We built this behind a counter, not in a boardroom

StockPilot started in a Sydney grocery shop that kept running out of the same four things. Six years later it runs in 340 shops around Australia.

Our story

It began with a notebook that never balanced

Our founder Reena Adhikari ran a grocery shop on King Street and counted her stock into a paper notebook every Sunday. The count was always wrong by Wednesday. The software she found was either free and useless or built for a warehouse with a full-time stock manager.

So she built something in between, and shops nearby started asking for a copy. That copy became StockPilot. We still test every new feature in a real shop before anyone else sees it, because a feature that only works on a quiet afternoon is not a feature.

What we care about

Three things we will not trade away

Your data stays yours

We do not sell what your shop sells. Sales figures are never shared with suppliers, competitors or advertisers, and you can export everything and leave whenever you want.

Privacy

Usable at 7am on a Monday

If a new casual cannot count a shelf without training, we have designed it badly. Every screen is tested with staff who have never seen the app before.

Usability

Honest pricing

One monthly price with the whole feature list included. No setup charge, no per-scan fee, and no lock-in contract that punishes you for changing your mind.
